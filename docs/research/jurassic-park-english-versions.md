# Warcraft III Jurassic Park: version identification research

## Chinese candidates for user recognition

Checked 2026-09-15 using visible Bilibili video titles/descriptions and an archived first-person guide. Videos were not watched in full, and no map was executed. Recognition cues below are not exclusive features or a verified change log. Do not assume Chinese 3.3 is identical to English Silver FINAL, or that Chinese 6.5 is identical to an EE release, without inspecting map files.

| Candidate | Recognition evidence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

A video is explicitly titled 3.3 final, but its exact G/H suffix is not confirmed. | [2009 guide, originally attributed to uuu9](https://wap.gamersky.com/gl/Content-144741.html); [3.3 final gameplay](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1uJj763Ez4/) |

Suggested recognition order: 3.3 and 6.5 first, then 6.2 or D98 depending on remembered scenes and character/dinosaur mechanics. This is an identification recommendation, not a claim about historical player counts. No reliable nationwide popularity ranking was found.

Another [video titled "most classic version, solo 真II 45 minutes"](https://www.bilibili.com/video/av251918247/) does not identify an exact map version in its visible description. Do not turn the difficulty label 真II into a version number.

## English archive research

Checked 2026-09-15. Read-only public map archive metadata and author/community threads. No map file was downloaded or played. Archive submission dates are not necessarily original release dates. Per-entry download counts do not establish Chinese player popularity.

## Early Survival 1.0 / 2.0

- [Jurassic Park Survival 1.0](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/958/): archive credits **Bawee**, submitted 2005-12-04, 14,156 downloads at inspection; description says **survive for 30 minutes**. File `Jurassic Park Survival 1.0.w3x` (0.77 MB), 128 × 128 map, playable 116 × 116.
- [Jurassic Park Survival 2.0](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/1110/): archive credits **hahaj**, submitted 2005-12-26, 31,096 downloads; also **survive for 30 minutes**. The differing author should not be silently merged into a single official lineage.

## Silver family: 3.2K / Silver FINAL

- [3.2K Silver](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/2195/): **PerfectlyInsane**, submitted 2006-04-20, 27,944 downloads. Description explicitly credits the original version to **Bawee**, gives **35–65 minutes** survival, and lists new dinosaurs, items, buildings, ending sequence, abilities and weapons.
- [Silver FINAL on Epic War](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/6550/): **PerfectlyInsane**, submitted 2006-09-27, 59,265 downloads. Same original-author credit and 35–65 minute survival; distinctive item text: **GPS @ Commercial Lab**. The displayed title is FINAL while the filename is `Jurassic Park Survival FINAL Beta.w3x`; do not infer a uniquely canonical final build from the title.
- [Silver FINAL author thread on Hive](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/jurassic-park-survival-silver-final.78786/): uploader/author PerfectlyInsane; created 2006-01-20, updated 2006-04-25; **7 players**. A contemporary March 2006 player requests longer than 65 minutes and mentions the helicopter timer and building an ultimate defence. These are community recollections, not executed rules verification.
- [3.2K.2 Silver on Hive](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/jurassic-park-survival-3-2k-2-silver.79051/): uploaded by knightzerox, embedded map author PerfectlyInsane. On 2006-04-26 PerfectlyInsane comments that a final version has been released, and discusses dinosaur models and harvesting wood. This distinguishes uploader from actual map author.

## Enhanced Edition (EE) family: 5.2 / 6.3 / 7.2b

- [EE v5.2 author page on Hive](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/jurassic-park-survival-ee-v5-2.120441/): **SaMmM**, created 2009-03-15, updated 2009-03-20, **7 players**. Page explicitly expands EE as **Enhanced Edition**. A 2009 player reports an electric-gate crash; another discusses the 6.2 version and surviving 60 minutes before reaching the helicopter. Treat those as community evidence, not authoritative rules.
- [EE v6.3 BETA](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/101119/): **SaMmM**, submitted 2009-06-30, 36,043 downloads; 3.19 MB. Description points to a then-official versions site but contains no usable mechanical changelog.
- [EE v7.2b](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/234510/): **SaMmM & qwerty-**, submitted 2013-09-29, 16,871 downloads; 3.24 MB. Later dated candidate, not evidence that this was the most played edition.

## Recommendation limits

For an English classic candidate, **Silver 3.2K / FINAL** is the strongest early historical anchor in these sources, with identifiable original-author credit and contemporary discussion. **EE 6.x** is a second plausible old survival family. The [Epic War Jurassic results sorted by downloads](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/?sort=downloads&order=desc&n=jurassic) place the Silver FINAL entry first in that archive query, followed by EE 6.3 BETA. This must not be restated as a global or Chinese popularity ranking. Chinese circulated map names/versions still need separate evidence and the user's recognition.
